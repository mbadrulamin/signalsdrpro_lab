# 🎞️ Fallback Recordings

> **What this folder is:** a short screen recording and a still picture of each demo, working.
> **Why:** if a demo fails on the day, you show its recording and move on within 30 seconds.
> **Who makes them:** you, at the rehearsal the night before. See
> [the setup checklist](../SETUP_CHECKLIST.md#24-record-a-fallback-for-every-demo-35-minutes).

Save them here with exactly these names. `../demo/preflight.sh` counts them.

| Demo | Still | Recording (30–60 s) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · See the invisible | `d1_spectrum.png` | `d1_spectrum.webm` |
| 2 · Hidden data | `d2_rds.png` | `d2_rds.webm` |
| 3 · Tour the busy bands | `d3_bands.png` | `d3_bands.webm` |
| 4 · Record now, analyse later | `d4_replay.png` | `d4_replay.webm` |
| 5 · Noise against a signal | `d5_bpsk.png` | `d5_bpsk.webm` |
| 6 · Television to your TV | `d6_television.png` | `d6_television.webm` |

💡 **Tip:** on Ubuntu, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd> starts and
stops a screen recording. It is saved in `~/Videos/Screencasts/`. Move it here and rename it.
For Demo 6, film the TV with a phone as well: the TV is the point.

⚠️ **Warning:** the recordings may be large. They are for this laptop, not for the repository.
Do not commit them.
