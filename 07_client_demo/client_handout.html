SignalSDR Pro

One box. Many radios.

What it is, what it does,
and what to do next

A software-defined radio is a radio whose job is decided by software — so one box can be many radios.

The box, in plain words

70 MHz – 6 GHzFrom FM radio to 5.8 GHz Wi-Fi and drone links
56 MHz at onceSee or record a whole band in one go
Transmit + receiveListen and send at the same moment
GPS-locked clockAccurate frequency and time anywhere
USB 3 to a laptopNo rack, no lab bench

What it is not

  • Not a certified electronic-warfare system
  • Not a direction finder on its own (one receive channel in this setup)
  • Not a code-breaker: it cannot read encrypted traffic
  • Not rugged: a lab, training and development tool
