1Install — once

# the toolkit, the driver, the maths library
sudo apt install -y gnuradio uhd-host python3-numpy
# the files the radio loads each time it starts
sudo uhd_images_downloader
# let yourself use the radio without sudo
sudo usermod -aG usrp,plugdev $USER

Then log out and log back in. The last line does nothing until you do. This is the most common reason a good setup "does not work".

2Plug in — in this order, every time

USB-C power
A-to-C cable→wait 30 s
it boots from its SD card→USB 3.0 data
blue port→antenna
on TX/RX, finger-tight

Not detected? Unplug both cables, wait, and start again from power.

3Check it works

uhd_find_devices     # the radio answers
uhd_usrp_probe       # look for "USB 3"
uhd_fft -f 98e6 -s 2e6 -g 40 -A TX/RX

The last one shows a live spectrum. Humps are FM stations. The thin spike in the middle is the radio's own leak — normal.

5The five mistakes everyone makes

  1. Gain at maximum. It raises the noise too, then distorts. Start at 40.
  2. No antenna, or the wrong one. The antenna matters more than the radio.
  3. Wrong sample rate. Rates must match along the chain — in Lab 01, 1,000,000 ÷ 20 = 50,000 for the speaker.
  4. Expecting a weak signal indoors. Buildings block radio. Try near a window.
  5. Skipping the fundamentals — then blaming the radio.

The words you heard today

Fifteen terms, in plain English. All 248 are in 05_reference/01_glossary.md.
github.com/mbadrulamin/signalsdrpro_lab
Turn the radio wave into numbers as early as you can — then do everything else in software.
TermWhat it means
IQTwo numbers per measurement: I "how far across" and Q "how far up". Together they tell how big the wave is and which way it is turning. Everything an SDR gives you is a stream of IQ pairs.
Sample rateHow many measurements per second. With IQ, the sample rate is the width of spectrum you see: 2 million per second shows 2 MHz.
MSPSMillion samples per second. "2 MSPS" = 2,000,000 measurements a second.
BandwidthHow wide a slice of spectrum is, in hertz (Hz). One FM station is about 200 kHz wide.
GainAmplification before the converter. Not volume. Too much adds noise and distortion; too little and the signal sinks into hiss.
dBFSHow full the converter is. 0 dBFS is completely full — the signal gets clipped (flattened). Stay well below it.
AGCAutomatic gain control. Keeps the level steady by itself, like a hand on a volume knob.
SquelchMutes the sound when no station is there, so you hear silence instead of hiss.
FFTThe maths that turns a signal into a spectrum: how strong it is at each frequency.
WaterfallA spectrum that scrolls downward over time. Bright stripes are signals; dark is empty air.
ConstellationA plot of the IQ points of a digital signal. Tight dots = clean. Clouds = noise. Clouds that touch = errors.
FlowgraphA GNU Radio program: blocks joined by arrows. You draw it; GNU Radio turns it into Python.
BlockOne step in a flowgraph — the radio, a filter, a decoder, the speaker.
DuplexSending and receiving. Full duplex = both at the same moment, which the SignalSDR Pro can do; half duplex = in turns.
Transport streamThe packet format digital TV uses: 188-byte packets carrying the picture, the sound and the channel list.

Three units on every screen

dBA comparison. +3 dB = twice the power. +10 dB = ten times.
dBmAn actual power. A strong FM station at your antenna might be around −40 dBm.
dBFSHow full the converter is. 0 dBFS is the ceiling you must not hit.
